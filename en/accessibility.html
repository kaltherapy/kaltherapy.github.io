Accessibility statement

I want everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use this site and book a session easily. The site was adapted according to the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013, and Israeli Standard 5568, based on WCAG 2.0 level AA, as far as possible.

What was done

  • An accessibility menu on the side of the screen: larger text, high contrast, highlighted links, a readable font and stopping animations.
  • The whole site can be used with a keyboard (Tab), with a clear focus outline and a "Skip to content" link on every page.
  • Clear heading structure, alternative text for images and labels for every form field.
  • The video has no sound and can be stopped with a button or from the accessibility menu.
  • The site works on phones, tablets and computers, and supports browser zoom.

Known limitations

The booking calendar is shown from Google Calendar, an external service I don't control. If it's hard to use, you can book by phone or WhatsApp and I'll gladly help.
