05NICE TO MEET YOU

My story

Ever since I was a kid I loved giving massages - to my grandfather and my dad, my siblings and my friends.

After October 7th I felt I wanted to help people, and I decided to study therapeutic touch. I believe pain in one part of the body isn't separate from the rest of the body, or from the mind. So I went to study holistic medical massage at Karkur College, and graduated in 2024.

After that I worked at a luxury spa in Caesarea. That's where I saw how much the quiet, the care and the small details matter - as much as the technique. I've been giving treatments for two years now, and today in my own clinic in Geva Carmel.

When I see people leave a session calm, loose, light and happy - I feel a deep sense of meaning and fulfilment.

07QUESTIONS

Before you come

Does it hurt?

The pressure is adjusted to you and can be changed at any moment. Working on tight spots can feel intense, but always within what's comfortable.

I'd prefer a female therapist. What's it like with a male therapist?

People ask me this a lot, and I completely understand. It matters to me that you feel comfortable from the moment you walk in until you leave.

We start with a few minutes of talking and decide together which areas to work on - and only those. You decide what to take off: underwear stays on, and you can also stay in light clothing. You're covered with a towel the whole time, and only the area I'm working on at that moment is uncovered. The chest and intimate areas are always covered.

During the session I ask how the pressure is and how you feel. If anything isn't comfortable, we can stop or change it at any moment, no explanation needed. And if you'd rather come with someone, that's completely fine.

Want to ask something before booking? Message me on WhatsApp.

How many sessions will I need?

Many people feel relief after the first session. For long-standing pain that keeps coming back, a few sessions in a row are usually worth it - that's what the series of 3 and 7 are for.

I have a medical condition or I'm pregnant. Can I come?

Tell me before the session. In some cases we'll adapt the treatment, and sometimes we'll ask for a doctor's OK first.

What should I bring?

Nothing. Towels and oils are at the clinic. Try to arrive a few minutes early, without rushing.

What if I need to cancel?

You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before the session, with a WhatsApp message. Later than that: 50% of the price. Illness, reserve duty or an emergency: no charge. Full cancellation policy.

Where is the clinic?

Derech HaArava 48, Geva Carmel - close to Atlit, Ein Hod, Tirat Carmel and south Haifa.
