Cancellation policy

I set aside a full 75 minutes for each client, and weekend spots are limited. Letting me know in advance means I can offer the time to someone else.

Cancelling or rescheduling

  • Up to 24 hours before: cancel or reschedule free of charge. A WhatsApp message is enough.
  • Less than 24 hours before: 50% of the session price (₪150).
  • No-show without notice: full price (₪300).
  • Exceptional cases such as illness, reserve duty or an emergency: no charge. Just let me know.

Arriving late

If you arrive late, the session still ends at the original time so the next client isn't affected, and the price stays the same.

Session series

  • A late cancellation (less than 24 hours) or a no-show counts as a used session from the series.
  • You can cancel a series at any time and get a refund for what wasn't used. Sessions already used are counted at the single-session price (₪300).
  • A series of 3 is valid for 3 months, a series of 7 for 6 months.

If I need to cancel

If I need to cancel or move a session, I'll let you know as early as possible and we'll find a new time together, at no cost.
